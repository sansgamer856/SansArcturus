# moka pot coffee

![Dish Image](image-link-here.jpg)

* **Prep time:** 15 mins
* **Cook time:** 30 mins
* **Servings:** 4

## Ingredients

- [ ] 2 cups flour
- [ ] 1 tsp salt
- [ ] 1/2 cup butter

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
2. Mix dry ingredients together in a large bowl.
3. Bake for 30 minutes until golden brown.
